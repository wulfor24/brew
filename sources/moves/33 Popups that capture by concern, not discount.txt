WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Arata gets roughly 650K visits a month and ships about 50K D2C orders. Even counting returning
buyers, that means around 92 out of every 100 visitors leave arata.in without buying. Today, as far as
we can see, nothing on the site asks those 92 people a single question before they go. No quiz. No
regimen builder. No subscription. No community. The brief lists all four as gaps. A popup system is the
cheapest way to close the first part of that gap, because it needs no new product, no new page and
no new app. It needs a few overlays, a set of rules and a reason for the visitor to say yes.
Assumption on current state: we have not been able to audit the live overlay stack, so we are treating
arata.in as running either no popup or a standard "sign up for 10% off" modal on entry. Either way the
fix is the same. A generic discount popup is the wrong tool for this brand. Arata's own reviews already
flag price ("a bit expensive in comparison", "on pricier side for a teenager like me"). A coupon on entry
confirms that the sticker price is negotiable and trains every new visitor to wait. The brand line is "zero
guesswork". The popup should do what the line says: ask the visitor what their hair is doing and hand
them a plan.
Every hero SKU carries a number tied to a timeline: 28,200 new hairs in 84 days, 87% saw growth in
84 days, 86% fewer greys over 3 months. That timeline is the asset a popup can sell. "Tell us your
concern and we will check on you at day 28, 56 and 84" is a stronger reason to hand over a WhatsApp
number than "10% off". It also sets up the 45-day reorder window the business depends on, and it
starts building the community the brief says does not exist.
There are three more specific costs of leaving the overlay layer empty. First, MitoActive launched in
August 2026 at ₹1,499 with zero reviews, and the thank-you page is the one moment where every
buyer is captive and willing. Second, the free shipping line sits at ₹299 and the free gift at ₹999, while
implied AOV is ₹900 to ₹1,000. A cart bar that shows the distance to ₹999 nudges a large share of

carts across that line. Third, the price objection on the ₹649 serum and the ₹1,499 MitoActive is
predictable, which means an exit-intent popup can answer it in one line (pack of 3 at ₹999 is ₹333 a
bottle; MitoActive is ₹50 a day) at the exact moment the visitor is leaving.
The opportunity, in plain numbers: if 60% of visits are new, 70% of those see the concern picker, and
6% opt in, Arata gains about 16,000 named contacts a month with a stated hair concern attached.
Those are assumptions and we say so in the measurement section. Even at half that rate it is the
largest owned-audience input the brand has, and it lands on the 32% of revenue that already runs on
the site Arata controls rather than the 68% on rented marketplace land.

1. Cart threshold bar (P1). What: a persistent bar in the mini-cart and cart page that shows distance to

2. Thank-you page offer (P2). What: a bottom sheet after order confirmation. MitoActive buyers see
   the first-500 programme (day 0 and day 84 photo, a WhatsApp check-in cadence, early access to

   WhatsApp opt-in. Why: MitoActive has zero reviews and this is the only place where 100% of its
   buyers pass. The reorder opt-in builds the 45-day repeat window into the customer's phone.
   Expected effect: 25 to 40% of MitoActive buyers join (assumption; thank-you page offers to
   confirmed buyers convert far above the 2 to 5% email popup norm), which puts the first batch of
   day-84 reviews on the PDP by early January 2027.
3. PDP exit-intent handler (P3) on two pages: Hair Growth Intensive Serum (₹649 → ₹571) and

   serum?". Expected effect: 3 to 6% of exit impressions click through to the pack-of-3 or the combo
   (assumption, within the skill's 3 to 10% exit-intent range).

    No popup fires on checkout, account, order tracking or policy pages.
    No popup fires on the first page view of a Google organic landing on mobile.
    Every capture popup shows consent copy and links the privacy policy; no pre-checked boxes.
    Every popup closes on X, outside tap, "Not now", and Escape.
    Focus is trapped while open and returns to the triggering element on close.

    the WhatsApp and email tools daily.
    Each popup sends impression, close, field-focus, submit-attempt and submit-success events to
    GA4 with the popup name and the concern tag.
