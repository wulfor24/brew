WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Arata is a D2C haircare brand, so we translate the community playbook before we use it. "Community"
here means a hair journey community: WhatsApp cohort groups, an Instagram broadcast channel, and
a graduate ambassador track, all built around the 84-day window every Arata claim already promises.
"Activation" means the customer actually uses the serum twice a day. "Churn" means no second
order by day 90. "Support deflection" means fewer "is this shedding normal?" and "my bottle leaked"
messages reaching the support inbox.
The brief lists "no community" as a known gap. We see it as the biggest missing piece in the retention
story, because the product is already shaped like a community program. Every hero claim is time-
bound. Up to 28,200 new hairs within 84 days. 87% saw growth with MitoActive in 84 days. 86% grey
reduction over 3 months. A customer who buys one 30 ml bottle, uses it for three weeks, hits the
normal week-two shedding, and quits, never reaches the number on the pack. That customer writes
"didn't work for me" on Nykaa and moves to Pilgrim at ₹595. The product did not fail. The 84 days did
not happen.
The cost shows up in the repeat rate. Beauty repeat rates sit around 26%; routine-led models exceed
40%. Arata ships 50,000+ D2C orders a month at an AOV around ₹900 to ₹1,000 with no subscription,
no reminder and no place for a customer to say "I'm on day 23 and I'm not sure." The habit the brief
describes is "buying whatever is on offer on Blinkit." A community that holds a customer for 84 days is
the cheapest way we know to break that habit, and it runs on a channel Arata owns outright.
Marketplaces and quick commerce take 68% of revenue on rented land. A WhatsApp community is
Arata's own land.

DECISION RULES
If it works (Day 84 completion above 30% and member repeat rate at least 12 points above the base
by day 90): open a grey cohort, hand two cohorts a month to graduate hosts, build the on-site "My
Journey" page, and move the Day 30 offer into a subscribe-and-save option for members first.
If it half works (check-in rates healthy, repeat rate flat): the loop is social but the offer is wrong. We
test the Day 30 bell against a Day 25 and a Day 40 version, and test Pack of 3 against MitoActive as
the default next bottle, before touching the community design.
If it does not work (check-in rate under 15% by week 6, staff writing more than 60% of posts): we stop
opening new cohorts, keep the MitoActive Lab Cohort alive for its reviews, fold the bells into the
lifecycle WhatsApp and email flows as 1:1 messages, and keep the Instagram channel as a content
surface. Arata loses one community lead's time, nothing else.

HELP DESK PINNED FAQ (FIRST SIX)
1. Seal broken or bottle leaked: reply LEAK with a photo; replacement ships within 48 hours.
2. Wet or dry scalp for the serum: dry. 4 to 5 drops along the parting and crown, twice a day. One 30
   ml bottle is roughly 30 days.
3. Shedding increased in week two: read the Day 14 pinned post; message the lead if still heavy at
   Day 35.
4. Intensive Serum and MitoActive together: yes, one morning, one night, or 5 minutes apart.
5. Pregnant or breastfeeding: ask your doctor; we won't advise.
