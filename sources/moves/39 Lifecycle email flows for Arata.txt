WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Arata is a D2C brand, so the lifecycle moves differently from a software product. Here is the
translation we use throughout: "signup" means an email or WhatsApp opt-in before the first order;
"onboarding" means the first 84 days of using the product; "upgrade" means moving from the ₹571
Intensive Serum to MitoActive at ₹1,499, or from a single bottle to the Pack of 3; "renewal" means the
reorder inside the 45-day window; "churn" means a customer who has not bought again by day 90.
Arata ships 50,000+ D2C orders a month and 1.5M customers a year walk through the brand. The brief
lists no subscription, no quiz, no community and no reminder to reorder. That means the moment a
customer finishes a 30 ml serum, nothing from Arata reaches them at the exact point of decision. They
go back to Blinkit and buy whatever is on offer. The brief says it plainly: habit is "buying whatever is on
offer on Blinkit". Email is the cheapest tool Arata owns to break that habit, and it owns the channel
outright. Marketplaces and quick commerce are 68% of revenue on rented land. The email list is the
one asset that does not pay a platform margin.
The product itself asks for a lifecycle program. Every hero claim is time-bound: 28,200 new hairs
within 84 days, 87% saw growth in 84 days, 86% fewer greys over 3 months. A customer who buys
one bottle and stops at day 30 never reaches the number on the pack. They conclude it "did not
work", write a lukewarm review, and go try Pilgrim. The 84-day window is a retention program already
written into the claims. Arata just has not sent the emails.
There are three more specific gaps email fixes faster than anything else. MitoActive has zero reviews
two months after launch at ₹1,499. A Day 30 review ask to every MitoActive buyer solves that in one
cycle. Packaging complaints ("the seal was broken", "the shampoo bottle was leaking") sit in public
reviews because nobody asked the customer privately first. A Day 2 delivery email with a no-
questions replacement promise moves those complaints into a support inbox. And the hair-fall buyer's
biggest fear ("will it make hair fall worse first?") is best answered on day 7, before they quit.

    If the 45-day repeat rate is up 5 points or more by day 90, we extend the routine flow to Nykaa and
