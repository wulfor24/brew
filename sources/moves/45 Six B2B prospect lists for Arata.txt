WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Prospecting is a SaaS word. For Arata it means one thing: building the account lists that feed B2B outbound.
The six segments are dermatologists and trichologists, salons, corporate wellness buyers, gyms, hostels and
co-living operators, and the category managers at quick-commerce and marketplace platforms. This
document builds those lists. The outbound copy, the sequences and the reply handling sit in the cold-email
document.
Arata already moves about 2 lakh B2B units a month against 50,000 D2C orders. That is a real number, and it
tells us the brand knows how to ship cases, not just parcels. What we cannot see in any public source is a
named-account B2B motion: no clinic programme, no salon programme, no corporate gifting page, no "for
professionals" page on arata.in. Assumption: today's B2B volume is mostly bulk orders from marketplace and
quick-commerce partners and distributors, not accounts that recommend or retail Arata to a patient, a client
or a member.
That gap costs Arata in two places. The first is revenue on rented land. Marketplaces and quick commerce
are 68% of FY25 revenue. Every clinic, salon, gym and hostel account is a channel Arata owns, with a
reorder rhythm (a 200 ml Anti-Hair Fall Intensive Shampoo lasts a salon's backbar roughly a week, a
MitoActive counter pack at a clinic turns monthly) and no platform taking a margin on the relationship. The
second is proof. MitoActive launched at ₹1,499 with zero reviews. "Clinical Kavya", 35, Chennai, has seen a
dermatologist and wants something a derm would respect. The fastest way to earn that respect at scale is
for 200 dermatologists to have MitoActive on the counter and the Y100 study summary in the drawer. A derm
who dispenses it is worth more to the D2C conversion rate than any ad.
The opportunity is sized by the market the brief already describes: 82 million urban Indians with hair loss, 5
million using medicated OTC, 1 million in clinical care. The clinical and near-clinical layer is exactly where
derm and tricho clinics sit. Salons and gyms touch the 16 million on basic retail products. Hostels and
corporate wellness reach the 22 to 40 cohort at the moment the trigger fires: a new city, hard water, a
stressful quarter.
One rule before any list gets built. A list without a buying signal is a phone book. Every account on these
sheets carries a signal, a date and a source URL, or it drops to Tier 3 where the segment definition does the
work.

COMPLIANCE FOR INDIA, READ BEFORE SOURCING
     The Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to named people. Business contact details collected from
     a business's own published pages for a business purpose are the low-risk path. We record source URL
     and collection date for every contact and honour any deletion request within 7 days.
     TRAI rules on promotional calls and SMS. We call published business lines only. No cold SMS to mobiles.
     WhatsApp outreach goes to published WhatsApp Business numbers, from a verified Arata business
     account, and stops on the first "stop".
     Doctors cannot endorse products publicly under the medical council's professional conduct rules. The
     derm play is counter dispensing, in-clinic recommendation and study education. We never ask a doctor
     for a testimonial, a quote or a video. That shapes which contacts we collect: clinic manager for
     commercial, doctor for science.
