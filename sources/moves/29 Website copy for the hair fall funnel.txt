HOUSE RULES FOR EVERY ARATA PAGE
These are the rules we write to and hand to Arata so every future page, listing and ad matches.
1. The claims grammar. Every number appears with four parts: the number, what was measured, the
timeframe, and the study type. The number goes in the headline. The other three go in the line under
it. Example: "Up to 28,200 new hairs" / "in 84 days, counted in a consumer study". This is how a
sceptic who has been burned by onion shampoo decides to trust the number. It also keeps the page
on the right side of the "clinical-strength" regulatory line the brief flags.
2. Outcome first, mechanism second. The reader buys less hair on the pillow. She reads about
Hairdian AP, rosemary-caffeine and Y100 only after she believes the outcome. Mechanism copy lives
one scroll below the hero on every PDP, under a heading that makes a claim ("Why the follicle stays in
growth longer"), never under "Ingredients".
3. One routine, one price, one CTA. Every concern page ends in a named routine with a single price.
"Hair fall routine: Anti-Hair Fall Shampoo + Growth Serum, ₹838." The reader should never have to
assemble a cart from a grid.
4. Indian hair is a proof point, so say what it means. Hard water in Bangalore and Delhi, humidity in
Mumbai and Chennai, pollution in the NCR winter. "Tested for Indian weather" is a claim; "holds
through a Mumbai August" is a picture.
5. The transition from "zero chemicals". The old identity shows up as a reader question ("Is it
chemical-free?"). We answer it once, in the FAQ, with facts: sulphate-free, paraben-free, vegan,
cruelty-free, dermatologically tested, every active there for a measured reason. The phrase
"chemical-free" itself does not appear anywhere on the site.
6. CTA formula. Verb + what they get. "Start my hair fall routine", "Add the 84-day pack", "Get the
routine for ₹838". "Shop now" and "Buy now" are retired.
7. Microcopy answers the review complaints. "Arrives sealed. If it does not, we replace it." sits under
every add-to-cart button. The cart drawer shows the gap to the ₹299 and ₹999 thresholds.
8. Words. Use: scalp, roots, follicle, 84 days, hair fall, new hair, tested, Indian hair, routine, lab. Avoid:
chemical-free, miracle, cure, regrow 100%, ayurvedic, clinical-strength.

PRE-PUBLISH SELF-CHECK (RUN ON EVERY PAGE)

   each hit against the banned patterns.

3. Count fragments and lists of three per section. One of each at most.
