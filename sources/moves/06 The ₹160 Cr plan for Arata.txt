WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Written for a D2C brand: in this plan, activation means the first purchase of a routine on arata.in or
quick commerce, retention means the reorder inside 45 days and finishing the 84 days, and referral
means the friend who notices the hair fall stopped.
Arata grew 192% to ₹61.1 Cr in FY25 and is forecasting ₹75 Cr for FY26. The stated goal is a ₹160 Cr
revenue run-rate with profitability. In plain numbers: ₹75 Cr is ₹6.25 Cr a month on average, and ₹160
Cr is ₹13.3 Cr a month. The business has to roughly double its monthly run-rate, and do it without
Traya's cost structure (₹138 Cr of marketing to make ₹343 Cr, and a ₹23 Cr loss).
The growth so far has been bought one bottle at a time. 68% of revenue sits on Amazon, Nykaa,
Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart, where the next sale depends on whoever is running the deeper
offer that week. There is no subscription. There is no quiz or routine builder. There is no 84-day
onboarding after the order, even though 84 days is the number on the bottle. MitoActive launched at
₹1,499 in August with zero reviews and still has zero. The pack-of-3 serum is 49% off while the actual
routine combo is 7% off, which trains the customer to stock up on one SKU instead of buying the
system. Beauty repeat rates run around 26%; routine-led models exceed 40%. Arata is selling a
routine and getting paid like a single-SKU brand.
That gap is expensive. At a 26% repeat rate, every rupee of acquisition has to be spent again for the
next order. At 40%+, the same ad budget produces a fifth more revenue over a year because the
second and third orders arrive without a CPO attached. Add the review-visible problems (leaking
bottles, broken seals, onion vs biotin and serum vs tonic naming across channels) and part of the paid
spend is re-acquiring customers the product experience lost.
The pieces already exist. A number on every bottle, an exclusive mechanism in Y100, an in-house lab,
2,248 site reviews, a 4.2 star Nykaa listing on 2,435 ratings, and a team that pulled off the Samay
Raina mock podcast. What is missing is the system that connects them in order. This plan runs the
workstreams in one sequence: fix the leak first (routine, reorder, proof), then pour (paid, creators,

THE PLAN
THREE BETS, IN ORDER OF IMPACT
Bet 1: Make the routine the product. The 84-day window is already on the bottle. We build the
business around it: an 84-Day Hair Fall Plan at ₹1,199 billed monthly, a MitoActive 84-day course at
₹3,999, Routine on Repeat subscriptions on six SKUs, a discount rulebook, and a 24-email lifecycle
from day 0 to day 84 and the reorder. Expected effect: repeat share of arata.in revenue from roughly
30% (assumption) to 45% by month 12. This is the largest lever in the plan because it changes what
every rupee of acquisition is worth.
