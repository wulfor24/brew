WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
A marketing loop is a job that runs on a schedule, checks one signal, and acts only when a real
threshold is crossed. For software that means churn watch and trial nudges. For Arata it means the
D2C equivalents: a 45-day reorder window, a Blinkit tile out of stock in Bangalore, a Meta ad shown to
the same person eleven times, a Nykaa review that says the seal was broken.
Arata is doing 50,000+ D2C orders a month on a ₹61 Cr base and chasing ₹160 Cr. At that volume the
question is no longer "what should we post". It is "what slipped this week that nobody saw". Every
known gap in the brief is a slow leak. No subscription and no reorder reminder, so the 45-day repeat is
left to the customer's memory. 68% of revenue sits on Amazon, Nykaa, Flipkart, Zepto, Blinkit and
Instamart, where a price mismatch or a stock-out is invisible from Delhi until the weekly sales report.
MitoActive is two months old at ₹1,499 with zero reviews. Leak and seal complaints keep appearing.
Naming drifts across channels (onion vs biotin, serum vs tonic).
What this costs is hard to see because nothing breaks loudly. A fatigued ad set keeps running and
pays 20% to 30% more per order for two weeks. A stock-out on quick commerce shows up as a
competitor's bottle in the cart. A customer who bought the Hair Growth Intensive Serum once and
never got a day-22 reminder does not complain. They join the 74% who do not repeat, in a category
where routine-led brands pass 40%.
The opportunity is that Arata's model is unusually loop-friendly. Every hero SKU has a fixed usage
period (30 ml at twice daily, 84-day course), so the reorder moment is predictable to the day. Every
claim comes from a study with a known design, so compliance can be a ledger. And the brand already
has 2,248 on-site reviews plus 2,435 Nykaa ratings to mine every week.
So the plan is eight loops, adopted in order, each with a trigger, a self-check, a memory and a stop
rule. Most runs find nothing and say so. The ones that find something stage a draft, an alert or a
recommendation for a named owner. Nothing spends, sends or publishes without a human until Arata

The Monday digest is the only loop that sees the whole board; it dispatches each notable mover to the
