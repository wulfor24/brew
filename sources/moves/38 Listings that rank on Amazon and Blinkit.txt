WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Arata has no app, so this discipline translates directly: app store listing optimisation becomes product
page optimisation on Amazon.in, Nykaa, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart. Same job. A
shopper types a problem into a search bar, sees a grid of tiles, taps one, reads for six seconds, and
buys or leaves. The title, the first image, the rating and the price do the selling before the page even
loads.
This is where most of Arata's money already lives. Marketplaces were 38% of FY25 revenue and
quick commerce was 30%. That is 68% of ₹61.1 Cr, roughly ₹41 Cr, earned on shelves Arata does not
own and cannot redesign. The only lever Arata controls on those shelves is the listing itself.
Today the listings do not carry the brand's strongest asset. Arata's edge is a number on every bottle:
7.4X, 28,200, 95%, 86%, 87%. On arata.in those numbers sit in the headline. On marketplaces the
naming is still inconsistent across channels (onion versus biotin, serum versus tonic). A legacy listing
we found on Purplle still calls the hero shampoo "Arata Natural Regenerating Anti-Hairfall Super Onion
Shampoo with Apple Cider Vinegar and Argan Oil". That is the 2021 brand talking, with "natural" and
"onion" doing the work that "7.4X hair fall reduction" should be doing. Nykaa's best-reviewed Arata
SKU is the legacy Super Shampoo (4.2★, 2,435 ratings) at ₹899, which the rebrand is moving away
from.
The cost is twofold. First, search. On Amazon and quick commerce, the title is the strongest ranking
signal, and a title built around "onion" ranks for onion-shampoo searches where Mamaearth and WOW
own the shelf at ₹250 to ₹350. It does not rank for "hair fall shampoo", which is the brief's own
customer language. Second, conversion. A shopper comparing Arata at ₹359 against Pilgrim and
Mamaearth sees "natural" on all three tiles and picks on price. A shopper who sees "7.4X hair fall
reduction" on one tile has a reason to pay more.
Then there is MitoActive. A ₹1,499 serum launched in August 2026 with zero reviews is invisible on
marketplace search and unconvincing on the page. On a 30 ml SKU priced at three times the
category, the review count is the listing. The opportunity is to use the review and search machinery of

REVIEW THEMES THAT SHAPE THE LISTING
From the brief's verbatim review data:
    Positive: "Arata is amazing cz its affordable and actually works", "Hair fall reduced drastically", "I
    can see a visible difference in two weeks", "Foams well and cleans my scalp off dirt and excess
    oil", "without any sticky residue or stiffness".
    Negative: "the seal was broken", "the shampoo bottle was leaking", "it doesn't have the box", "a bit
    expensive in comparison to other competitive haircare lines".
Three listing consequences. One, "visible difference in two weeks" and "two to three months" need to
be in the bullets and images as the expected timeline, so a week-one buyer does not leave a 2★
review. Two, leak and seal complaints need a public seller response template and a packaging image
showing the seal, because an unanswered leak review costs more than the refund. Three, the price
objection gets answered on the page with the compare: ₹359 against ₹595 to ₹699 for the same
category.
