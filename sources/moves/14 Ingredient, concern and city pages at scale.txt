WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
Programmatic SEO for a SaaS company means integration pages and "CRM for real estate" pages. For
Arata it means ingredient pages, hair-concern pages, city and water-quality pages, and comparison
pages, built from one template each and one data table each.
Arata gets about 650K visits a month and that number is growing 42% month on month. Most of that is
paid, creator and quick-commerce spillover. The brief shows no evidence of an organic engine. Lab
Notes exists, but seven posts landed in March 2026 and then the cadence stopped. There is no page
on arata.in that ranks for "post-partum hair fall", "hard water hair fall", "what is Y100" or "Arata vs
Traya". Those searches happen every day, and right now Traya, Pilgrim, Healthline and Reddit answer
them.
That costs Arata in two places. First, acquisition: every hair-fall customer who lands on a Traya blog
post enters a funnel that ends with a ₹1,400 a month kit. Second, trust: MitoActive has zero reviews
and a ₹1,499 price. A buyer who searches "Y100 hair growth" today finds Yuva Biosciences press
releases, not Arata. The brand that owns the science has no page explaining it.
The opportunity is unusually good because Arata has the one thing most pSEO projects lack:
proprietary data. Study numbers on every SKU (7.4X, 28,200, 87%, 95%, 86%). 2,248 on-site reviews
tagged by product. 50,000 D2C orders a month with a pin code on each one. An in-house lab in Delhi.
Pilgrim and Minimalist can copy an ingredient page. They cannot copy "what Arata's hair-fall
customers in Bangalore actually buy".
The plan below builds 160 pages across 5 sets. Each page has a reason to exist, a data source that
nobody else has, and a route to a cart. We ship 48 in the first 30 days and the rest over 90.

BUILD PLATFORM: STAY ON SHOPIFY
Arata runs Shopify. The pages stay there. Subfolders on arata.in, never a subdomain, because every
ranking signal must accrue to the domain that also hosts the PDPs.
The build uses Shopify metaobjects. One metaobject definition per set (concern, ingredient, city,
comparison, routine). Each entry is a row. Each definition gets the web-pages capability so each entry
renders at /[definition]/[handle]. One Liquid template per definition with conditional sections that
branch on field values. Data enters by CSV import and updates through the Admin API from a Google

1. The first 120 words are written by hand for every page. No page ships with a generated intro.

   wash advice; men-skewed concerns show the Intensive Serum first, women-skewed show the
   shampoo and conditioner first.
4. Each page recommends at most three products. The recommendation changes by page or the
   page does not ship.
5. If two pages would recommend the same products with the same reasoning, merge them.
   Cannibalisation check runs before build, not after.

Y100 is an active that works on the energy supply of the hair follicle.
Most hair growth actives you have heard of do one of two things. Minoxidil widens blood vessels.

the cell that makes energy. Follicles with better energy supply spend longer in anagen, the growth
phase, and less time in telogen, the resting phase. More hairs growing at once reads as more density.
