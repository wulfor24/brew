WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ARATA
In software, a launch means opening signups. For Arata a launch means a product that is on the shelf,
in the cart and in the reviews. So we translate "beta", "early access" and "Product Hunt" into customer
cohorts, seeded dermatologists and creators, and the new-launch slots on Amazon, Nykaa, Blinkit and
Zepto.
MitoActive launched on 12 August 2026 with a press story and a ₹1,499 price. Two months later it has
zero reviews on arata.in. The Hair Growth Intensive Serum next to it has 474 reviews, and its pack of 3
has 388. A customer who lands on the hair growth collection sees a ₹1,499 serum with nothing beside
it and a ₹571 serum with hundreds of people vouching for it. The page argues against your hero.
The claim window causes this. The headline claim is "87% saw increased growth in 84 days". Nobody
reviews an 84-day product on day 7, and the review request most Shopify stores send at day 7 lands
at the exact moment the customer has nothing to say. The first customers who bought on launch day
reach day 84 around 4 November 2026. That date is three weeks away. Nothing is planned for it.
The rebrand is the second launch, and it is a site launch. arata.in does 32% of revenue, about 650K
visits a month and 50,000+ D2C orders a month. A rebrand that renames products changes product
handles, which changes URLs. Naming is already inconsistent across channels (onion vs biotin, serum
vs tonic), the curl-care range still sits on marketplaces under the old identity, and "zero chemicals"
copy still exists in places. Shipping a new brand without a redirect map, a name map and a
marketplace sync costs organic traffic that took two years to earn.
The opportunity is that Arata launched once and stopped. The ₹160 Cr goal needs the brand to launch
again and again: MitoActive results, the rebrand, the second biosciences serum, the new seal, a
routine builder. Each one is a moment. Each one should end with more people on your WhatsApp and
email lists, so that the 68% of revenue sitting on rented marketplaces slowly comes home.

    Product renames on Shopify change the handle. Every old URL needs a 301 to the new one, or
    Google drops the page.
    Collection pages for the curl-care range exist on marketplaces and probably in Google. A sunset
    without redirects creates 404s on ranked pages.
    "Zero chemicals" and "chemical-free" copy on Amazon and Nykaa listings contradicts the new
    positioning the day the site changes.
    Quick commerce dark stores hold old packs. For a few weeks a customer who sees the new pack
    on Instagram receives the old pack from Blinkit. Without a line on the pack or in the order message,
    that reads as a counterfeit.
    Analytics and the Loox review widget are the two things most often broken by a theme change.

LAUNCH 1: THE MITOACTIVE PROOF LAUNCH (THE 84-DAY COHORT)
What it is. 500 customers start MitoActive on the same week, on a 3-bottle, 84-day course, with
check-ins on WhatsApp at day 0, 28, 56 and 84. They send photos. We send the next bottle, the
routine and the study context. At day 84 their reviews, photos and numbers become the results
launch.
Why it works. It fixes Lovable (500 real voices at the price point) and Complete (the course has a
start, a middle, an end and a reorder built in). It builds the first owned community. It pilots the
subscription Arata does not have, because bottles 2 and 3 ship on a schedule. And it gives a date to

launch towards.
Phase 1, internal (week 1 to 2). Thirty Arata staff and friends start the course. They test the WhatsApp
flow, the photo instructions, the bottle 2 shipping trigger, and the seal on this SKU. The leak
complaints in reviews come from shampoo bottles, but a ₹1,499 serum that leaks ends the launch. We
also write the first honest explainer of Y100 in customer words.
Phase 2, alpha (week 2 to 3). Pull every MitoActive buyer since 12 August from Shopify. Message
them on WhatsApp and email: you are at day 50 to 60, tell us what you see. Move the Loox review

Phase 3, beta (week 3 to 4). Open 500 spots. Invite order: the 388 pack-of-3 reviewers first (they
finish courses and they write), then Intensive Serum buyers in the last 120 days, then waitlist. Spots
close when full, and the number stays visible. The kit, the price and the guarantee are in the worked
examples.
Phase 4, early access (week 5 to 10). Seed 50 dermatologists and trichologists in Delhi, Mumbai,
Bangalore, Hyderabad and Chennai with the 84-day kit plus the study summary. Seed 20 hair-fall
creators (men's grooming and women's post-partum, not beauty generalists) with the same kit and
the check-in schedule. Nothing is paid; they run the course and we ask for honesty. Leak content
weekly: Lab Notes "Cohort week N", day-28 photos with consent, the most common question that
week. Run a short survey at day 28 (how disappointed would you be if MitoActive disappeared) to
sharpen messaging before the results launch.
Phase 5, full launch (mid January 2027). Day 84. Reviews publish in one batch so the product page
goes from 0 to hundreds in a day. The results page goes live with the cohort's own numbers. Email,
WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube and press carry the same three figures. The Amazon and Nykaa
listings update with the review count and a results image. The MitoActive 84-Day Pack becomes a
permanent SKU with scheduled shipping. Cohort 2 opens the same day.
Expected effect. MitoActive moves from 0 to 300+ on-site reviews, the hair growth collection gets a
hero that out-sells the ₹571 serum on margin, and Arata has an owned group of 500 people who will
try the second biosciences serum first.

LAUNCH 2: THE REBRAND GO-LIVE
What it is. A dated switch of identity, site theme, product names and packaging, run as a site launch
with QA in three passes, and synced to marketplaces and quick commerce the same week.
Why it matters. The rebrand is the brand's biggest story of the year, and the only thing that can make
it a bad story is a broken site or a confused shelf.
Sequence.
1. Name map first (week 2). One canonical name per product. A table of old name → new name → old
   URL → new URL → every channel where the old name appears. The curl-care range gets a
   decision: fold into Styling, or sunset with redirects to Damage Defence.
2. Copy sweep (week 3). Every "zero chemicals", "chemical-free" and legacy claim found across
   arata.in, Amazon, Nykaa, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart, with the replacement line.

3. QA pass 1, before the switch (week 4 onward on the staging theme). The checklist is in the
   appendix. The three items that fail most often: production theme carrying a noindex, the review
   widget missing on the new product template, and conversion tracking firing on the button rather
   than the order.
4. Go-live day (December, date set by Arata). Redirects publish with the theme. Top 10 pages checked

Decision rules.
    If the cohort fills in under 7 days, open a second 500 immediately rather than waiting for results.
    Demand is the proof.
    If the cohort fills slower than 250 in 14 days, the price or the ask is wrong. Test ₹3,499 with the
    shampoo removed before touching the promise.
    If day-84 results come in under 70% reporting growth, publish them anyway, with the study
    number beside them, and shift the hero message from "growth" to "hair fall reduction" if that
    number is stronger. The brand line is honest results. The cohort is only worth running if that holds.
    If organic traffic drops more than 10% in rebrand week 2, roll the theme back (Shopify: publish the
    previous theme) and fix the redirect map before republishing. Reputation can wait a week. Rankings
    cannot.
    If MitoActive reaches 300 reviews and 25% of collection revenue by the end of January, the same
    cohort method becomes the launch template for the second biosciences serum, with the first 500
    cohort members invited first.
