# 08 · Demo guide

## Before the call (5 minutes)

- [ ] Open https://demo.joinsquareup.com. Arata shows "Fully loaded".
- [ ] Open the feed once. Cards load, and the story rings show items.
- [ ] Make one image draft in a jam. It shows Arata's real product. If it says the image budget is used up, the shared daily limit has been hit; ask engineering.
- [ ] Have the prospect's own URL ready if you plan a live read on brew.joinsquareup.com. It takes about 3–4 minutes and costs about $6.

## The 10-minute story

1. **"Know what to do next."** Open the demo picker and pick Arata. Choose **Start the tour** (about 4 minutes) or **Jump to the feed**.
2. **The read is real.** Show what Brew found about Arata:
   - **664 live Meta ads**, and rivals' ads too;
   - about **79k reviews and ratings** (say "reviews and ratings", not "reviews");
   - **named in 12 of 32 AI answers, and 0 of 20 when the shopper doesn't name the brand.**
3. **The moves.** Open the Moves tab. Show one move PDF: the depth, the sources and "to confirm". Say "strategy documents", not "51 moves" (there are more).
4. **The feed.** Switch views (Founder → Ads → Storefront) and show how the cards change. Point out:
   - a **Heard** card (real people's words, with links);
   - an **Idea** card (a ready post, and where to post it);
   - a **story** ring.
5. **The jam.** Open a card that shows "First draft". Ask the brief for a change. Make an image or a carousel. Show the versions.
6. **Share the brief.** Tap **Share with the ShopOS team**. Show the copied brief with its BR number and the read-only page. **"This is how work comes to us."**
7. **The bridge to working together.** Connect a simulated account (Shopify) and show the Dummy cards appear: **"Once you connect your real accounts, these are your numbers."**

## Say this / not this

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Brew drafts it; your team or ours ships it from the brief" | "Brew launches it" |
| "Once connected, this is where your Shopify numbers go" (pointing at the Dummy tag) | "These are your numbers" |
| "Every number has a source, and anything unsure says 'to confirm'" | "Everything is verified" |
| "Your teammates can jam here; today their replies are simulated" | "Your team approved it" |

## Avoid in this version

- **"Ask everyone"** in a jam. Its answers are invented and not labelled.
- **"Take it to Slack".** It says "Posted to #growth", but nothing is posted.
- **Festival cards after 27 Nov 2026**, when the dates go stale.

## If something breaks

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Cards look thin, stories empty | The treg balance ran out, or just after a deploy | Refresh in a minute; ask engineering to check treg |
| "Budget for today is used up" | The shared daily limits were hit | Use existing drafts; ask engineering |
| Reading screen spins forever | The store blocked us | Use the demo brand instead |
