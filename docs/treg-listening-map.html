Listening everywhere, measured in numbers

Where treg plugs into each step of the Brew loop, so every move in the feed comes with counts (how many people, how much search, how much money) and quotes (what they actually said, with links).

Prices are treg list prices per call on 14 Oct. "Works" rates are treg's measured success across real calls. No paid calls were made for this audit.

The loop

Every stage gets its own data

  1. GatherListen everywhere + quant sizing, on a cadence
  2. MovesSkills turn evidence into move docs
  3. Rank → feedSize × proof × fit × past lift
  4. JamHooks, creators, drafts, assets
  5. ExecuteDo it now, or brief to the team
  6. MonitorRe-run the same probes after launch
  7. MeasureDelta vs baseline, graded A–D
  8. LearnLift per move family feeds ranking

Where we stand today

The Arata build in your screenshot went further (Nykaa, Flipkart, Instagram). treg already powers AI-answer checks, Google Trends, Meta ad library and Amazon in research and the reader. Stages 2–3

From evidence to ranked moves

Move families and the data behind each

  • Listening: themes with counts across sources → reply, FAQ, PDP and ad-angle moves (customer-research, copywriting).
  • Rival weakness: rival 1–3★ reviews + rival ad comments → comparison and "switch" moves (competitors, ad-creative).
  • Demand: volume × CPC × gap → SEO, collection and AI-SEO moves (seo-audit, ai-seo, programmatic-seo).
  • Creative: top ads + viral videos + transcripts → hook and UGC moves (ad-creative, video).
  • Community: live threads and questions → post and reply moves (community-marketing, social).
  • Creators: people already posting about the category → seeding moves (influencer-marketing).
  • Price and offer: Shopping + Amazon position → offer moves (offers, pricing).

Rank score, all from real inputs

  • Size: people reached (mentions, search volume) and money at stake (volume × CPC, or AOV × orders affected).
  • Proof: number of independent sources, recency, and whether the quant and the qual agree.
  • Timing: trend slope from weekly Trends and mention counts.
  • Fit: can it run on the brand's connected stack today.
  • Past lift: this family's graded results for this brand, then across all brands.

Every move doc shows its numbers and at least two linked quotes. A move with no evidence never reaches the feed.

Stages 4–5

Jam and execute

In the jam

  • Hooks from the category's top TikToks and reels, with view counts, shown next to the draft.
  • Creators: influencersclub.creators.search ($0.006) for a shortlist; full audience analytics ($0.48) only after the brand picks someone.
  • Assets: Seedance, Veo, Gemini Image, GPT Image and voice are all in treg (the make-ugc skill already chains them).
  • Quotes from listening inserted as proof lines in copy, linked to the source.

Do it now / brief to team

  • Execution stays on the brand's own connectors (Meta, Shopify, Klaviyo, email/WhatsApp via Brew).
  • treg does not post into communities, and neither does Brew. Community moves stay copy + open thread.
  • At launch Brew saves a baseline snapshot of every probe tied to the move, so measuring has a "before".
Stages 6–8

Monitor, measure, learn: re-run the same probes

Each move family gets a fixed probe that ran at launch and runs again on a schedule. The difference is the result. This fills the families in measure-families.js with outside evidence, not only store numbers.

Build order

What I would build first

  1. Listening sources: add TikTok, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Facebook comments, Trustpilot, Google reviews and news to listen-watch.js. Move YouTube to treg. Turn X and Amazon on by default under the cap. Each source shows on the Where list with its count, as in your screenshot.
  2. Quant layer: a new market-pulse.js that sizes each listening theme (volume × CPC, trend slope) and stores it in the pack, so every move and card can quote it.
  3. Evidence-based rank: rank score from size × proof × timing × fit × past lift, replacing the model's guess.
  4. Baseline at launch + probes: snapshot on Do it now / report back; scheduled re-runs per family; deltas into the measuring engine and What Brew learned.
  5. Jam inputs: hooks from top videos and ads, creator shortlist, proof quotes.
  6. Indian marketplaces: structured extract until treg adds a native endpoint (request #1171).
